App Overview

AutoCopier is a macOS code snippet manager with a unique auto line-by-line copy feature. It helps developers and power users save, organize, and rapidly paste multi-line code snippets. Simply press Cmd+V repeatedly and AutoCopier will automatically feed the next line into your clipboard.

All data is stored locally on your Mac. No cloud, no accounts, no tracking.

How Auto Copy Works

1

Open a snippet and click "Auto Copy By Line"

2

The first line is copied to your clipboard automatically

3

Switch to your target app and press Cmd+V to paste

4

AutoCopier detects the paste and loads the next line into clipboard

5

Repeat Cmd+V until all lines are pasted. The editor closes automatically when done.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does AutoCopier need Accessibility permission?

AutoCopier monitors the Cmd+V keyboard shortcut system-wide to detect when you paste. This allows it to automatically load the next line into your clipboard. The permission is only used for this purpose and no keyboard input is recorded or transmitted.

Where is my data stored?

All snippets are stored locally on your Mac at ~/Library/Application Support/AutoCopy/snippets.json. No data is sent to any server or cloud service.

Is AutoCopier free?

Yes, AutoCopier is completely free to use with all features available.

Can I back up my snippets?

Yes. Your snippets are stored as a standard JSON file. You can back up or transfer the file at ~/Library/Application Support/AutoCopy/snippets.json.

The auto copy feature is not working?

Make sure you have granted Accessibility permission. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility and ensure AutoCopier is enabled. You may need to restart the app after granting the permission.
